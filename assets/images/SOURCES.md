# Product image sources

These placeholder photos are from Unsplash and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Photographer credits and original photo pages are listed below.

Each filename prefix has a `-355.webp` version (355 × 355 pixels) and a `-710.webp` version (710 × 710 pixels). The larger version supports a 355px display slot at 2× pixel density; the smaller version also covers the approximately 158px mobile slots.

Images were cropped individually to square compositions and resized with Lanczos resampling, then encoded as WebP at quality 82 using method 6. Downloaded source JPEGs were requested at up to 2000px wide and are not included in this repository. No output was upscaled. Source metadata was omitted from the generated files.
